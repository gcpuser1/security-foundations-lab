# 04 · Radio and telecoms

**Priority:** High (added) · **Effort:** ~2 week(s) · **Status:** not started

## Why this matters

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and mobile networks all start as radio waves. Your electronics and communication degree is the lever here.

## 1. Stumble first (before any reading)

Open a Wi-Fi analyser app at home and note which channels your neighbours use. Predict why some channels are crowded and others empty, then test your guess.

Write your guess in `notes.md` before you look anything up.

## 2. Concepts covered

- [ ] Electromagnetic waves, frequency, bands (2.4/5/6 GHz)
- [ ] Modulation and OFDM
- [ ] Channels, interference, signal-to-noise ratio
- [ ] Bluetooth basics
- [ ] 4G/5G architecture at a high level

## 3. Build yourself

An RTL-SDR receiver with SDR++ to watch the 2.4 GHz band as a waterfall (receive-only, your own equipment).

## 4. Practise on

Wi-Fi analyser on your own network.

## 5. Gate check

Explain how OFDM splits a Wi-Fi channel into subcarriers, using your signals degree.

Explain it to Claude with no notes. Don't start the next unit until you pass.
