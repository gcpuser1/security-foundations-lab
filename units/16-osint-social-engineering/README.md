# 16 · OSINT and social engineering

**Priority:** High · **Effort:** ~2 week(s) · **Status:** not started

## Why this matters

People are the weakest link. Studied from the defender's side, this unit shows why trust fails and how processes stop it.

## 1. Stumble first (before any reading)

Search for yourself the way a stranger would. Before you start, predict what you'll find. Compare.

Write your guess in `notes.md` before you look anything up.

## 2. Concepts covered

- [ ] The intelligence cycle and source verification
- [ ] Your own digital footprint
- [ ] Cialdini's principles of influence
- [ ] Phishing and pretexting patterns
- [ ] Verification processes and phishing-resistant MFA

## 3. Build yourself

An audit of your own digital footprint, plus a verification process (callback checks, passkeys) for your household or team.

## 4. Practise on

Trace Labs Search Party CTF (OSINT for missing persons) and Bellingcat's investigation guides.

## 5. Gate check

Match Cialdini's principles to real phishing examples, with the control that defeats each.

Explain it to Claude with no notes. Don't start the next unit until you pass.
